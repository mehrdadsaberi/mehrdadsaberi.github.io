<h2 id="about">About Me</h2>
<p class="bio">
I recently completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by <a href="https://www.cs.umd.edu/~sfeizi/">Prof. Soheil Feizi</a> in the Reliable AI Lab, and defended my dissertation on <b>November 9, 2026</b>.
My research focuses on <b>reliable and safe AI</b>: detecting AI-generated images and text, <b>watermarking</b> and data provenance, <b>machine unlearning</b> in large language models, and the <b>robustness</b> and interpretability of generative models.
I also come from a competitive programming background (IOI Silver Medalist).
</p>
